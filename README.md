# HR Employee Attrition Analysis & Data Visualization

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes employee attrition patterns using an HR employee dataset. The analysis explores factors such as overtime, departments, job roles, age, monthly income, and employee experience to identify patterns associated with employee turnover.

The project also includes a professional Python-based visualization dashboard that summarizes the main findings.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze overall employee attrition
- Explore attrition across departments and job roles
- Examine the relationship between overtime and attrition
- Compare monthly income across employees who stayed and left
- Identify important patterns through data visualization
- Present findings through a professional dashboard
- Provide data-informed recommendations for HR decision-making

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## 📊 Visualizations

The project includes:

- Overall Employee Attrition
- Attrition Rate by Overtime
- Attrition Rate by Department
- Attrition Rate by Job Role
- Monthly Income by Attrition
- Correlation Analysis
- Distribution and outlier analysis
- Final HR Employee Attrition Dashboard

## 📈 Key Findings

- Overall employee attrition in the dataset is 16.1%.
- Employees working overtime show a higher attrition rate than employees who do not.
- Attrition varies across departments and job roles.
- Monthly income patterns differ between employees who stayed and those who left.
- Employee experience and income show a strong positive relationship.

## 💡 Recommendations

- Monitor overtime and workload patterns.
- Investigate job roles with relatively higher attrition.
- Compare retention patterns across departments.
- Review compensation and career progression.
- Consider multiple employee factors together when investigating attrition.
